What itgraph is

itgraph is a private analytics tool developed and operated by an individual developer (GitHub: vyhuholl). It follows a hand-reviewed set of public YouTube and Telegram channels about IT and sends alerts about unusually well-received videos and posts to a private Telegram chat. Using itgraph means reading or acting on those alerts. Access is by the developer's invitation only.
